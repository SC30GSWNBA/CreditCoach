# CreditCoach User Guide: Creating Your Account and Your Weekly Questions

This guide is for anyone using CreditCoach for the first time. It walks you through creating an account, logging in, and how the limit of 5 questions a week works. The second half is a list of common questions.

CreditCoach is a financial-literacy project. It explains credit scores in plain language. It is not financial advice, and it is not connected to your bank or to any credit bureau.

## 2. Before you start

**The profile CreditCoach shows you is a practice profile, not your real credit report.** CreditCoach does not look up your real score. It builds sample cards, loans and a sample 12-month score history that fit the answers you give, so the coaching feels like it is about someone in your situation. Every number you see is generated.

Because of this:

- You never need to give exact amounts. Every question is answered by picking a range.
- Nothing you do in CreditCoach can affect your real credit score.
- For any real decision, check your official credit report or speak to your lender.

**You need a current web browser** (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) on a computer or phone.

## 3. Create your account

Open the CreditCoach link. The first page shows how many questions CreditCoach has answered so far (it updates by itself) and how many people have signed up. Below them are two large buttons: **Log in** and **Sign up**. The one you are on is shown in solid indigo.

### Step 1: Open the Sign up tab

Press **Sign up**. You will see a short introduction and a section called **Your answers stay private**. Please read it; it is also summarised in [section 9](#9-your-privacy).

### Step 2: Tell CreditCoach what to call you (optional)

- **What should CreditCoach call you?** A nickname is fine, up to 30 characters. If you leave it blank, CreditCoach shows your user number instead.
- **Gender** is optional. You can pick "Prefer not to say" or skip it.

### Step 3: Answer the required questions

There are 10 required questions in two sections. Each one is marked with a red asterisk (**\***), and each section has a line under its heading saying it is required.

**About you**

| Question | Why it is asked |
|---|---|
| Which age group are you in? | Sets a realistic age for your profile. |
| How long have you been working full-time? | Sets how large your practice credit limits are. |
| How many credit cards do you currently have? | Sets how many cards your profile has. |
| Which best describes your current credit score? | Sets roughly where your practice score sits today. |

**Cards and loans**

| Question | Why it is asked |
|---|---|
| How do you usually pay your credit card bill? | Shapes the month-by-month story of your score. |
| In a typical month, how much of your total card limit do you use? | Sets the balances on your practice cards. |
| In the last 12 months, how many times have you paid late? | Adds late payments to your score history if you had any. |
| Which of these do you currently have? (loans) | Adds each loan you select to your profile. You can select more than one. |
| Have you ever considered a payday loan, an instant cash app loan, or a paid "credit repair" service? | Lets CreditCoach talk to you about risky products if they are relevant. |
| What is your biggest financial goal for the next 2 years? | Gives CreditCoach a goal to plan around. You can select more than one. |

Tips for answering:

- **Pick the closest option.** There are no right or wrong answers, and "I don't know" is always fine where it is offered.
- **If you have no credit card**, choose "0" for the number of cards. You can then skip the two card questions (how you pay, and how much of your limit you use); CreditCoach fills them in as "not applicable" for you.
- **If you have no loans**, tick "No loans".
- **Each option is a box you click.** The one you pick turns indigo.

### Step 4: Answer the optional questions (if you like)

Three more sections are collapsed below the required ones. Click a heading to open it.

- **How you follow your score**: 4 questions.
- **A 6-question credit quiz**: a quick check of what you already know. Your answers are not marked in front of you.
- **Saving and how you split your salary**: 4 questions.

These add detail to your profile. Skipping them does not limit what you can ask later.

### Step 5: Enter your invite code (only if you see the box)

If the form shows an **Invite code** box, sign-up is by invitation. Type the code you were given by the person who invited you. If there is no such box, you do not need a code.

### Step 6: Tick the box and create your login

At the bottom, tick:

> I understand that the scores and accounts CreditCoach shows me are a practice profile built from my answers, not my real credit report.

Then press **Create my CreditCoach login**.

**If something is missing**, nothing is created. A red box appears under the button listing exactly which questions still need an answer, or asking you to tick the box. Fix those and press the button again.

## 4. Save your User ID and password

When your account is created, a pop-up appears titled **Your CreditCoach login is ready**. It shows two things, one above the other:

- **User ID**, for example `creditcoach_user16`
- **Password**, 16 characters, for example `CL^70_6ccX%s&30!`

> **This is the only time your password is shown.** CreditCoach keeps only a scrambled copy of it, so nobody, including the CreditCoach team, can show it to you again or reset it.

### Do one of these before you continue

**Option A: Copy both.** Each box has a small copy icon in its top-right corner. Click it, then paste into a password manager or a note you will keep. Copy the User ID and the password separately.

**Option B: Email them to yourself.** If the pop-up shows a **Backup (optional)** line, type your email address and press **Email me my login**. You will get one message with the subject "Your CreditCoach login" containing your User ID and password.

- Your address is used for that one message and is not stored.
- Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within a minute or two.
- If you see "The email couldn't be sent", copy the details instead.
- If there is no Backup line, email is not available on this copy of CreditCoach; use Option A.

Doing both is the safest choice.

### Then confirm and continue

Tick **I have saved my User ID and password** and press **Continue to Log in**. The pop-up will not close until you tick the box.

### About your password

- It is exactly 16 characters and completely random.
- It always contains at least one lowercase letter, one uppercase letter, one number and one special character (such as `!`, `@`, `#`, `%`, `&`, `_` or `?`).
- It is **case-sensitive**. `A` and `a` are different.
- Some characters look alike (`0` and `O`, `1` and `l`). Copy and paste instead of retyping.

### If you lose your password

There is no "forgot password" option. If you did not copy your password and did not email it to yourself, that account can no longer be opened. You will need to sign up again, which creates a **new** User ID with a new profile. Your earlier conversations stay with the old account.

## 5. Log in

After you press **Continue to Log in** you are taken to the **Log in** tab. A green note confirms that your login was created, and your User ID is already filled in.

1. Check the **User ID** box shows your User ID.
2. Paste your **Password**.
3. Press **Log in** (or press Enter).

CreditCoach opens and shows "Signed in as …" at the top, with how many of your questions it has answered so far.

**Coming back later:** open the same link. If you are still logged in on that browser you go straight in. Otherwise you see the Log in tab; enter your User ID and password.

**If the login is refused** with "That User ID and password don't match":

- Paste the password instead of typing it, and make sure no space was copied before or after it.
- Check the User ID is all lowercase with no spaces, like `creditcoach_user16`.

**After 8 wrong attempts in 15 minutes**, that User ID is locked for the rest of the 15 minutes and you will see "Too many attempts for this User ID". Wait, then try again with the correct password. This protects your account from guessing.

## 6. Find your way around

Once logged in you will see:

- **Signed in as …** and a **Log out** button at the top.
- **Questions answered for you so far**, across all your sessions. It goes up as soon as an answer arrives. It is a count of answers, not of the questions you have left this week (that is under each answer; see section 7).
- **Your memory and past conversations**: click to open. It shows the goal CreditCoach has saved for you and your earlier conversations.
- Three large tabs: **Ask CreditCoach**, **My credit** and **Dashboard**.

### Ask CreditCoach (the chat)

Type a question in the box at the bottom and press Enter, or click one of the 12 example questions under "Go ahead, try CreditCoach". Clicking an example sends it straight away, so it counts as one of your questions.

Each answer shows:

- Small badges at the top saying what CreditCoach's safety checks did and whether the answer was reused from an earlier identical question.
- The answer itself, using your practice score history and accounts.
- A **confidence percentage** with the reason for it. This shows how well the answer is backed by your data and CreditCoach's library. It is not a guarantee that the answer is right.
- The library sources it used.
- **Questions left this week**, with the date and time your week resets.

**When something can't be reached.** CreditCoach still answers, and tells you what it couldn't do:

- **Your data can't be loaded right now:** it says it can't pull your latest data and suggests trying again shortly. It never guesses a figure. If it read the same figure earlier in the same conversation, it may repeat it, marked as *last confirmed* with the time, because it may have changed since.
- **It has nothing in its library on your topic** (for example buy now, pay later): it says so instead of making up rules or fees, and may add general advice that does apply.
- **You ask how CreditCoach is built** (its design, code, AI model, database or hosting): it says it can't go into that, then offers to help with your credit and answers any credit part of your question.
- **You ask about "this loan" or "that offer" without saying which:** it asks what the loan is (the kind of loan and the lender) and its terms (amount, interest or fees, repayment period) before giving a view.

When your data couldn't be loaded, the question **doesn't count** towards your 5, and the line under the answer says *"This one wasn't counted"*. The other two are full answers, so they count (see section 7).

**Tell us how the answer was.** Under each answer are 👍 and 👎. If an answer was wrong, slow or hard to follow, press 👎 and pick the reason that fits best: *Wrong or missing figure*, *Didn't answer my question*, *Too slow* or *Hard to follow*. The project team reads every rating with the answer it belongs to, and uses them to decide what to fix. Rating an answer doesn't use up a question.

### My credit (your charts)

This tab shows your latest score, how it changed, your card utilization, what you owe, and a month-by-month "score story" with the reason for each change. You can switch between 3, 6 and 12 months and try a "what if I pay down a card?" slider.

**Using My credit never uses up a question.** Picking a month and pressing **Ask in chat** only places a question in the chat box; it is not sent, and not counted, until you press Enter.

**If you told CreditCoach you have no cards and no loans**, you have no credit file yet. My credit will say so instead of showing charts, and the chat can explain how people start building credit.

### Dashboard (how CreditCoach is doing)

This tab shows how CreditCoach is performing for everyone who uses it: how often its data lookups fail, how often its safety checks step in (by rule), and how often it remembers a saved goal. Pick the last day, week or month, and real use or the team's test runs. It shows **counts only**: never anyone's questions, answers or name. Using it never uses up a question. Right after you log in it may say "Loading the dashboard…" for a few seconds while it gathers the latest figures. **Real use** counts only answers people got in the app; the note under the figures says how many more are under **Eval and test runs**.

Pick **Cost and speed** above the figures to see what answers cost and how long they take: spend, cost per answer, response time, a chart of every answer's cost against its wait (untick models to compare just some; the number of answers, the median and the average on the chart change to match), each model compared, and where the time and money go. It also shows the team's comparison of models on the same 50 test questions. Like the rest of the tab, it shows counts only.

## 7. Your 5 questions a week

Every answer CreditCoach writes has a real cost, so each person who signs up can ask **5 questions per week**. This keeps CreditCoach available to everyone testing it.

### How your week works

- **Your week is your own.** It starts the moment you ask your first question, not on a fixed day like Monday.
- **It lasts exactly 7 days** (168 hours) from that moment.
- **When it ends, you get 5 new questions.** Your next week starts when you ask your next question after that.
- **Unused questions do not carry over.** If you ask 3 this week, next week you still have 5, not 7.

**Example.** You ask your first question on Tuesday at 3:10 PM. You now have 4 left, and your week resets the following Tuesday at 3:10 PM. If you use all 5 by Thursday, you wait until Tuesday 3:10 PM. If you then ask your next question on Wednesday at 9:00 AM, your new week runs until the Wednesday after that at 9:00 AM.

### Where to see how many you have left

Under every answer you will see a line like:

> Questions left this week: **3 of 5** · resets Tue 20 Oct, 03:10 PM IST

The reset time is shown in CreditCoach's server time zone, with the zone's abbreviation after it.

### What happens when you reach the limit

If you ask a sixth question, CreditCoach does not answer it. Instead you see:

> You've used all 5 questions for this week. Your next 5 unlock on **Tue 20 Oct, 03:10 PM IST**. Until then the **My credit** tab still shows your score story and accounts.

You stay logged in, and everything else keeps working: My credit, your memory panel and your past conversations.

### What counts as a question, and what does not

| Counts as 1 question | Does not count |
|---|---|
| Any question CreditCoach answers | Sending an empty message |
| A follow-up question in the same conversation | An answer that fails with "Sorry, I couldn't reach the answer service" |
| Clicking one of the example questions | The "You've used all 5 questions" message itself |
| A question CreditCoach declines to answer directly, such as a request to guarantee a score, to recommend a payday loan or to explain how CreditCoach is built. It still replies with an explanation, so it counts. | Anything on the My credit tab |
| A question you have asked before, even if the answer is reused | Opening your memory and past conversations |
| | An answer that says your data couldn't be loaded right now ("This one wasn't counted") |
| | Logging in or out |

If CreditCoach cannot check your allowance for a technical reason, it tells you so, does not answer, and does not count the question. Try again a little later.

### Getting the most from 5 questions

- **Look at My credit first.** It is unlimited and often answers "what is my score?" or "how much do I owe?" without spending a question.
- **Ask one complete question** instead of several short ones. "Why did my score drop in August, and what should I do first?" is one question.
- **Tell CreditCoach your goal** once ("I want to reach 750 by next June to buy a car"). It remembers it and plans around it in later answers.

## 8. Staying logged in, and logging out

- **You stay logged in for up to 7 days** on the browser you used, even if you close the tab.
- **Press Log out** at the top when you are finished, especially on a shared computer. You return to the welcome page.
- **You may be logged out early** if CreditCoach is restarted for an update. Just log in again; nothing is lost.
- **Being logged out does not change your weekly questions.** The count belongs to your account, not to your browser session. Logging out and in again, using another browser, or using another device does not reset it or use any up.
- **You can log in on more than one device** with the same User ID and password. They share the same 5 questions.

## 9. Your privacy

- **No name, phone number, PAN, bank login or email is needed** to sign up. You answer in ranges, never exact amounts.
- **Your answers are used only to build your practice profile**, so that the coaching feels personal.
- **CreditCoach never sells your answers or passes them** to lenders, banks, credit bureaus, employers or advertisers.
- **To write an answer, your question and practice profile are processed by the AI model service CreditCoach runs on** and kept in CreditCoach's own database. Nothing in them says who you are.
- **Your conversations are saved** so CreditCoach can remember your goal and pick up where you left off. The project team can read them to check that answers are accurate.
- **How each answer was built is recorded** (which library passages and tools it used, how long each step took) in a monitoring service the team uses to find and fix mistakes, together with any 👍 or 👎 you give.
- **Your password is stored only in scrambled form.** Nobody can read it back.
- **If you use the email backup, your address is not stored.** It is used to send that one message.

**Please do not type real personal details into the chat**, such as your full name, phone number, PAN, Aadhaar or card numbers. CreditCoach hides common identifiers it recognises before saving or processing a message, but the safest approach is not to enter them at all. It never needs them to answer.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

### Creating an account

**Do I have to pay?**
No. CreditCoach is free.

**Do I need an email address or phone number?**
No. Email is only an optional way to send yourself a copy of your login.

**Why does CreditCoach ask these questions?**
They are the same questions the team used in user interviews. Your answers decide what your practice profile looks like: how many cards and loans it has, how full the cards are, and how the score moved over the past year.

**Will signing up affect my real credit score?**
No. CreditCoach never contacts a bank or credit bureau and never applies for credit.

**Is the score I see my real score?**
No. It is a generated practice score on the 300–900 scale used by Indian credit bureaus, placed in the range you said best describes your score.

**I pressed the button and nothing happened.**
Look under the button for a red box. It lists the required questions that still need an answer, or asks you to tick the box above the button.

**I have no credit card. What do I choose?**
Choose "0" cards. The two card questions are then answered "not applicable" for you.

**I chose the wrong answer. Can I change it after signing up?**
Not at present. Your profile is built once, at sign-up. If it matters, sign up again; you will get a new User ID and profile.

**I am under 21 or over 42. Which age group do I pick?**
Pick the closest one: "21 to 25" if you are younger, "Above 35" if you are older. It only sets an approximate age on your practice profile.

**What is the invite code?**
On some copies of CreditCoach, sign-up is limited to invited people. If you see an Invite code box, enter the code from the person who invited you. If the code is refused, check it with them.

**It says "Sign-ups are full for now."**
CreditCoach accepts a limited number of sign-ups in total. That limit has been reached, so new accounts cannot be created at the moment. If you already have a User ID, you can still log in.

**It says sign-up isn't available on this copy of CreditCoach.**
That copy is not connected to its database. Logging in with an existing User ID still works. Please let the team know at [creditcoachurcreditmentor@gmail.com](mailto:creditcoachurcreditmentor@gmail.com).

**It says "Sorry, we couldn't create your login just now."**
Nothing was saved. Wait a moment and press the button again; your answers are still on the page.

**Can I choose my own User ID or password?**
No. User IDs are numbered in the order people sign up (`creditcoach_user16`, `creditcoach_user17`, …), and passwords are generated randomly so that every account has a strong one.

**Can I create more than one account?**
Each sign-up creates a separate account with its own profile and its own conversations. Please keep to one, because the number of sign-ups is limited and each extra account takes a place someone else could use.

### Your User ID and password

**I closed the pop-up without copying my password.**
The pop-up will not close until you tick "I have saved my User ID and password", so this should not happen by accident. If the browser was closed or the page reloaded before you copied it, and you did not email it to yourself, the password is gone. Sign up again.

**I forgot my password. Can you reset it?**
No. CreditCoach stores only a scrambled copy, so it cannot be shown, emailed again or reset by anyone. If you used the email backup, look for the message with the subject "Your CreditCoach login". Otherwise, sign up again.

**I forgot my User ID.**
If you used the email backup, it is in that message. Otherwise it cannot be looked up, because CreditCoach holds nothing that connects an account to you.

**The email did not arrive.**
Check your spam or junk folder and that you typed the address correctly. You can press **Email me my login** again while the pop-up is still open. Once the pop-up is closed, the email cannot be sent, because the password is no longer available.

**Is it safe to email my password?**
The email is a convenience for a practice account that holds no real financial data. It contains your User ID and password in plain text, so treat it like any message containing a password: keep it in an inbox only you can open.

**Can I change my password?**
Not at present.

**Can I share my login with a friend?**
It is better for them to sign up themselves. A shared login shares the same 5 questions, the same profile and the same conversation history.

### Weekly questions and resets

**How many questions do I get?**
5 per week.

**When does my week start?**
When you ask your first question. It is not tied to a calendar week.

**When exactly do I get more questions?**
7 days (168 hours) after the first question of your current week. The exact date and time are shown under every answer and in the message you see when you reach the limit.

**Do I have to wait until midnight or Monday?**
No. The reset is at the same time of day as your first question, 7 days later.

**I used all 5 on the first day. How long do I wait?**
Until 7 days after the first of those 5. Using them quickly does not make the wait longer or shorter.

**Do unused questions carry over?**
No. Each new week starts at 5.

**Does logging out, closing the browser or switching device reset the count?**
No. The count belongs to your account.

**Does the count reset when CreditCoach is restarted or updated?**
No. It is stored with your account.

**Does a follow-up count as a new question?**
Yes. Every message CreditCoach answers counts as one.

**CreditCoach refused to answer what I asked. Did that use a question?**
Yes, if it replied with an explanation, for example when asked to guarantee a score or recommend a payday loan. Those replies are full answers that explain why and offer safer alternatives.

**I asked the same question twice. Did both count?**
Yes, even though CreditCoach may reuse its earlier answer for the second one.

**I got an error instead of an answer. Did that use a question?**
No. If you see "Sorry, I couldn't reach the answer service just now", the question is handed back to you.

**Can I see how many I have left without asking a question?**
The count is shown under each answer, so scroll up to your most recent answer. It is not shown anywhere else at present.

**Can I get more than 5?**
Not through the app. The limit is the same for everyone who signs up.

**What can I do while I wait?**
Use the **My credit** tab, which is unlimited, and read back through your earlier answers in **Your memory and past conversations**.

### Logging in and sessions

**How long do I stay logged in?**
Up to 7 days on the same browser, unless you press Log out or CreditCoach is restarted.

**I was logged out unexpectedly.**
CreditCoach was probably restarted for an update. Log in again with your User ID and password. Your profile, conversations and question count are unchanged.

**It says "Too many attempts for this User ID."**
There were 8 wrong passwords in 15 minutes. Wait until the 15 minutes have passed, then log in with the correct password.

**It says "Please sign in again to continue."**
Your session ended while the page was open. Reload the page and log in.

**Can I be logged in on my phone and my laptop at once?**
Yes. Both use the same account and the same weekly questions.

### Your data

**Does CreditCoach remember what I told it?**
Yes. It saves your conversations and the goal you state, and uses them in later answers. You can see what it holds in **Your memory and past conversations**.

**Can I delete my account or my conversations?**
There is no button for this yet. Email [creditcoachurcreditmentor@gmail.com](mailto:creditcoachurcreditmentor@gmail.com) from any address with your User ID, and the team will remove it; see [section 11](#11-getting-help).

**Who can see my answers and conversations?**
The CreditCoach project team, for checking accuracy and improving the product. They are not shared outside the project, and nothing in them identifies you unless you type it in yourself.

## 11. Getting help

CreditCoach is being tested with a small group. If something does not work, or you want your account removed, email the CreditCoach team at **[creditcoachurcreditmentor@gmail.com](mailto:creditcoachurcreditmentor@gmail.com)** and include:

- your User ID (never your password),
- what you were doing,
- the exact message you saw, and roughly when.

**Never share your password with anyone**, including the CreditCoach team. They do not need it and cannot use it to look anything up.

The team can help with problems and remove an account on request. It cannot recover or reset a password, or look up a forgotten User ID.
